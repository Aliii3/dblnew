About the mentor
Radwa Fathi

The Bold Architect of Egyptian Brands.

She’s not just the CEO of DOUBLESHOT Performance Marketing ,she is the pulse of Egypt’s marketing evolution. With 20+ years of strategy under her belt and a heart that beats for local brilliance, Radwa blends analytical precision with soul-stirring creativity. She's the marketer who dreams in SOSTAC, plans in BCG matrix, and executes with laser-sharp performance metrics, all while baking nostalgia into modern dessert brands and coaching the next Generation to rise as fearless innovators. Whether it’s fast-moving trends, game-changing campaigns, or a standout product launch reel, she’s all in, and always real. Radwa doesn't just build campaigns; she builds movements, sparking unity among local brands, reimagining family care through marketing, and mentoring young minds, even her 11-year-old junior marketer! to chase purpose over popularity.
